Fixo helps you take control of your finances with effortless expense tracking. Organize spending by categories and wallets, get clear insights into where your money goes, and build better financial habits — all in a clean, intuitive interface.

Key features:

- Organize spending with custom categories
- Filter and search your transaction history
- Manage an emergency fund
- Simple, distraction-free design
